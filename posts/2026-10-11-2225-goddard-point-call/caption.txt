Referee Marc Goddard is standing by the point he took from Christian Leroy Duncan for poking Brendan Allen in the eye at UFC Vegas 122.
Fair or harsh? 👇

Goddard says he warned Duncan in round 1 and again before round 2. Without the deduction the cards would have been tied before Allen's round 5 submission, and CSAC's Andy Foster wants stricter eye-poke penalties after what happened to Tom Aspinall. UFC news.
