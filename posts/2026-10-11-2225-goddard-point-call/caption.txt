Referee Marc Goddard is standing by the point he took from Christian Leroy Duncan in the UFC Vegas 122 main event.
Fair or harsh? 👇

Goddard says he warned Duncan about extended fingers in round 1 and again before round 2, then deducted a point when the eye poke landed. Without it, the cards would have been tied before Brendan Allen's round 5 submission. UFC news.
