Former UFC bantamweight champion Merab Dvalishvili says he and a friend were attacked from behind by Usman Nurmagomedov and others in Abu Dhabi.
Merab is in town for RAF Abu Dhabi, where he's set to wrestle Marlon Moraes this weekend. Video he shared shows both men bloodied and getting medical attention. No response from Usman's camp, the UFC or RAF has been reported yet.

Wishing Merab and his friend a full recovery.
