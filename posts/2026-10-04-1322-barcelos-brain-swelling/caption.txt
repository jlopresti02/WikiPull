UPDATE: Glover Teixeira says Raoni Barcelos' collapse against Raul Rosas Jr. was caused by swelling in the brain.
Retire or return? 👇

Barcelos went down in the fifth round of the UFC Vegas 121 main event and was stretchered out. Teixeira said he was not knocked out but blacked out, and Barcelos, 39, is reported to be in good spirits after leaving the hospital. Dana White has said the focus is on his health, not his fighting future. UFC news.
