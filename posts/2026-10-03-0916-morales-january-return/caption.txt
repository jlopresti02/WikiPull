Unbeaten Michael Morales says neither Carlos Prates nor Ian Machado Garry wants to fight him, so he's going another way to reach Islam Makhachev.
Duck or bad timing? 👇

The 19-0 UFC welterweight contender, ranked No. 3, says he has accepted a fight for January 2027 against an opponent he hasn't named, after the UFC tried for December. He calls it his last fight before a title shot and is targeting June 2027 for a crack at champion Makhachev. Morales hasn't fought since last November and says the UFC hadn't given him a fight in a year.
