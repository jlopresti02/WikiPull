Former UFC middleweight Tim Kennedy was reportedly shot in an ambush in the Democratic Republic of the Congo on Sept. 17.
Per reports, he was flown back to the U.S. and is in stable condition. A member of his team, former New Zealand SAS soldier Justin Conaglen, was reportedly killed. Details of the mission have not been confirmed.

Wishing Tim Kennedy a full recovery. Our thoughts are with Justin Conaglen's family and friends.
