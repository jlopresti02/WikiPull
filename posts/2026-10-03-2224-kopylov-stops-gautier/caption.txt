Ateba Gautier, one of the UFC's most hyped prospects, got stopped by Roman Kopylov in the first round at UFC 332.
Hype or real? 👇

Kopylov, who went in as the clear underdog, picked off Gautier with counters, dropped him with a one-two and finished with strikes to open the UFC 332 main card in Salt Lake City. Afterward Kopylov said it felt like he was dreaming. UFC 332 results and MMA news.
