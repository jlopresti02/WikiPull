UPDATE: Dana White admitted referee Herb Dean is having a rough year after King Green's UFC 332 loss was waved off.
Early or fair? 👇

Dean stopped Esteban Ribovics vs. King Green in round one after Green was dropped for a third time. Green said he had told Dean before the fight to let it go until someone got knocked out, and admitted he's a little mad. White said at the post-fight press conference that Green went down three times in a row and he has seen worse stoppages. It adds to a run of officiating debates around Dean this year.
