King Green could be one win away from a BMF title shot, and Dana White is on board.

At the UFC 332 press conference, White said he "absolutely" agrees that Bobby "King" Green is the right guy to fight for the BMF belt if he beats Esteban Ribovics on Saturday in Salt Lake City. Green, 40 and on a four-fight win streak, called the BMF title his dream but said he never thought it would happen.

The lightweight scrap was already heated: Ribovics called Green "Minnie Mouse" at the presser.
