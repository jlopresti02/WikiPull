Kayla Harrison just made Amanda Nunes tap to keep her UFC title.
New GOAT or rusty Nunes? 👇

Harrison finished Nunes with a rear-naked choke in round 4 at UFC 334 at Madison Square Garden to retain the women's bantamweight belt. Afterward she said she has big decisions to make about her future.
