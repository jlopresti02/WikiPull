Former UFC title challenger Anthony Smith was finished in his bare knuckle MMA debut, and he's out of a $500,000 tournament.
Shocked or not? 👇

PFL veteran Jordan Heiderman took Smith down and stopped him with punches at 2:17 of round two at Gamebred Bareknuckle MMA 12 in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, on Friday night. The heavyweight tournament bout headlined Jorge Masvidal's Gamebred FC card on DAZN, and the loss knocks Smith out of the bracket. Bare knuckle MMA results and fight news.
