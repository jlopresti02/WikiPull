Joe Rogan wants the oblique kick out of MMA.

On JRE MMA Show #186 with UFC fighter Sean Sharaf, the UFC commentator argued that oblique and side kicks to the knee should be banned, comparing them to eye pokes: both can cause permanent damage, and you can't tap out of a kick that wrecks a knee. He pointed to Jon Jones' fights with Thiago Santos and Rampage Jackson as examples. Sharaf was more on the fence, saying it's a realistic fighting technique. The debate heated up after CSAC executive director Andy Foster floated a ban under the Unified Rules following UFC 331, and Dana White has said the UFC doesn't disagree. 🦵
