UPDATE: King Green's BMF title dream took a hit as Esteban Ribovics stopped him one day after missing weight.
Fair or not? 👇

Ribovics, who came in half a pound over the lightweight limit, dropped Green three times and got the TKO at 4:08 of round one at UFC 332 in Salt Lake City. He apologized to the crowd for the weight miss after the win. Green had been on a four-fight win streak, and Dana White said this week a win could put him in the BMF picture.
