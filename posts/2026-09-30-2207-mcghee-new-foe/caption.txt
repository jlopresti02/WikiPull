Marcus McGhee has a brand-new opponent just three days before UFC 332.

Bernardo Sopaj is out of Saturday's fight with an injury, so the UFC called on newcomer Anthony Romero. The 26-year-old Black House MMA product is 7-2, holds the UNF bantamweight title and just defended it with a 25-second knockout. On such short notice, the bout moves up to featherweight.

McGhee, No. 15 at bantamweight, now faces a short-notice debut on the UFC 332 card at the Delta Center in Salt Lake City on October 3, headlined by Natalia Silva vs. Wang Cong for the vacant flyweight title. 🔁

Does McGhee handle the short-notice danger, or does Romero steal the spotlight in his UFC debut?
