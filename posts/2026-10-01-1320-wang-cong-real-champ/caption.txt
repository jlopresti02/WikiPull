Wang Cong says she won't need a win over Valentina Shevchenko to call herself the real UFC flyweight champion. 🏆

Two days out from UFC 332, Wang told MMA Junkie that beating Shevchenko isn't what would make the title legit for her. She meets No. 1 contender Natália Silva for the vacant women's flyweight belt in Saturday's main event at the Delta Center in Salt Lake City.

The title opened up after an injured Shevchenko vacated it in September. Silva rides a long unbeaten run into the title fight, while the former kickboxer Wang brings a four-fight win streak. Big UFC news for fight week either way.
