Aljamain Sterling isn't on board with banning oblique kicks.

California State Athletic Commission executive director Andy Foster has proposed outlawing the kick to the knee under MMA's Unified Rules, and Dana White has said the UFC doesn't disagree with his concerns. Speaking with Ariel Helwani, the former UFC bantamweight champion argued referees should enforce the fouls already on the books before adding new rules, and that head trauma is a far bigger safety issue than leg injuries.

Sterling (who meets Kevin Vallejos at UFC Qatar on Nov. 21) isn't alone: Thiago Santos, hurt by Jon Jones' oblique kicks at UFC 239, also opposed a ban. A big MMA rules debate heading into the end of 2026. 🦵
