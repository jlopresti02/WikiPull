Rafael dos Anjos left his gloves in the Octagon after Alexander Hernandez stopped him at UFC 332.
Retire or one more? 👇

The former UFC lightweight champion won the first round in Salt Lake City, but Hernandez dropped him with a jab early in round two and finished him with a hook and ground strikes at 3:12. It was the 41-year-old Brazilian's 50th pro MMA fight and his first in nearly two years, and it extends his losing skid to four. No retirement has been formally announced yet.
