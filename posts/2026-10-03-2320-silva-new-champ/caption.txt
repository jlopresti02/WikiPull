UPDATE: Natalia Silva shut up Wang Cong's fight-week trash talk and is the new UFC women's flyweight champion.
Shevchenko or Grasso? 👇

Silva won a unanimous decision (49-46, 48-47, 48-47) in the UFC 332 main event in Salt Lake City, dropping Wang with a head kick in round two before Wang rallied late in rounds four and five. The Brazilian becomes only the fourth women's flyweight champion in UFC history and stays unbeaten in the UFC. The belt was vacant after Valentina Shevchenko gave it up due to injury.
