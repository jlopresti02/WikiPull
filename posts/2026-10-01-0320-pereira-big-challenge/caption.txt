Alex Pereira says his next fight is on the way, and he's calling it a big challenge.

In a new Instagram story, the former two-division UFC champion told fans good news is coming. Pereira, now the No. 3-ranked UFC heavyweight, hasn't fought since a TKO loss to Ciryl Gane in the interim heavyweight title fight at UFC Freedom 250 in June. 🇧🇷

No opponent or date is official yet. Some outlets have floated No. 2-ranked Sergei Pavlovich as a likely matchup, while Gane defends the interim belt against Josh Hokit at UFC 334 on Nov. 14. Pereira has said he wants to become the UFC's first three-division champion.
