🇦🇺 Robert Whittaker is fighting in Sydney for the first time since 2014, and the UFC handed him Bogdan Guskov.
Whittaker or Guskov? 👇

The former middleweight champ meets the No. 10 light heavyweight in the UFC Sydney co-main event in February at Afterpay Arena. Whittaker beat Nikita Krylov in his 205 debut; Guskov is coming off a loss to Magomed Ankalaev.

🔥 Hot take: Beat Guskov and Whittaker is a real light heavyweight contender.
