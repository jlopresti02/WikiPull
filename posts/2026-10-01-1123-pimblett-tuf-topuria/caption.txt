Paddy Pimblett wants to settle his Ilia Topuria feud on The Ultimate Fighter. 🍿

Pimblett pitched coaching a TUF season opposite Topuria, saying six weeks side by side would let him get inside his rival's head, the way Conor McGregor built up his fight with Jose Aldo. The bad blood goes back to their 2022 hotel run-in at UFC London, and Pimblett first floated the TUF idea back in 2023.

Topuria, coming off a loss to Justin Gaethje at UFC White House, has said he's medically cleared to return this month. A big UFC lightweight rivalry still waiting for a fight announcement.
