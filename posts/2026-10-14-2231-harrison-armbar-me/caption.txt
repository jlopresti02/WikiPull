Kayla Harrison says she'd beat a prime Ronda Rousey.
Kayla or Ronda? 👇

The UFC bantamweight champ said on the Deep Waters podcast that Rousey ran through women new to MMA, though she credits her for paving the way. Rousey beat Harrison twice in judo when Kayla was 15; Harrison now defends her title against Amanda Nunes at UFC 334 at MSG on Nov 14.
