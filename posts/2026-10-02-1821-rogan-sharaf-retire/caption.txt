Joe Rogan told Sean Sharaf to seriously think about retiring, just weeks after Sharaf's shocking knockout of Gable Steveson.

Sharaf, a former U.S. Marine who came into UFC 331 winless in the UFC and a huge underdog, handed the Olympic gold medalist his first MMA loss. On the JRE MMA Show, Rogan said there's no shame in leaving on a high and urged Sharaf to let the moment sink in before deciding. Sharaf has said he's weighing retirement but hasn't set a timeline or a next fight.
