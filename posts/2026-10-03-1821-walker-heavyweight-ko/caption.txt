Johnny Walker moved up to heavyweight and knocked Mick Parkin out cold at UFC 332.
Title threat or not? 👇

The Brazilian landed an elbow and a jumping knee in the clinch before a second knee put Parkin down at 3:35 of the first round in Salt Lake City. Walker then leapt out of the cage to celebrate with his wife. It was his heavyweight debut after years at light heavyweight, and a statement win on the UFC 332 prelims. UFC results and MMA news.
