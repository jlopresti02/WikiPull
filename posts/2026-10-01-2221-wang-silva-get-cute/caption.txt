Wang Cong says Natália Silva won't help sell their UFC 332 title fight, and Silva isn't playing along.

At Thursday's UFC 332 press conference in Salt Lake City, Wang said she even wrote Silva a note with an idea to spice up their ceremonial weigh-in faceoff, but Silva never looked at it. Wang's point: fighters need to build hype and bring money to the show, per MMA Fighting.

Silva's answer was short. She said Wang can get cute on her own, because this fight is her dream and she came to fight, not to play. The two meet Saturday at the Delta Center for the vacant UFC women's flyweight title.

Who's right here, Wang or Silva?
