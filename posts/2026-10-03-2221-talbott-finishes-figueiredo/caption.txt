UPDATE: Payton Talbott answered Deiveson Figueiredo's fight-week shove with a first-round TKO at UFC 332.
Contender or not yet? 👇

Talbott dropped the former UFC flyweight champion with a right hand early, then finished it after a knee in the bantamweight co-main event in Salt Lake City.
