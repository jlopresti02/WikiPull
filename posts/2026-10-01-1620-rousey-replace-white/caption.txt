Could Ronda Rousey be the next face running the UFC? Dan Hardy thinks it's worth a look.

The former UFC welterweight title challenger, now working with PFL Europe, said Dana White looks more focused on Zuffa Boxing and Power Slap these days, that the UFC largely sells itself now, and that the promotion would benefit from fresh leadership. Rather than pointing to an executive like Hunter Campbell, Hardy named UFC Hall of Famer Ronda Rousey as a possible successor.

This is one ex-fighter's opinion, not a UFC plan. White is still the UFC CEO, and Rousey hasn't said she wants the job. 👀
