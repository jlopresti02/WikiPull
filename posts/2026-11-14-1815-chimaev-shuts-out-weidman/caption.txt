Khamzat Chimaev shut out Chris Weidman 10-0 to win an RAF title in Abu Dhabi.
Chimaev in his prime or Weidman past his? 👇

The UFC star won by tech fall to take the RAF Light Heavyweight Crossover Championship. Weidman took the match on short notice after Colby Covington pulled out.
