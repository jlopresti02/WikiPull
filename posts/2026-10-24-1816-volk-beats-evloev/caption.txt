Alexander Volkanovski just handed Movsar Evloev his first loss to keep the UFC featherweight title.
Featherweight GOAT: Volk or Aldo? 👇

The champ finished the previously unbeaten Evloev with punches in round 2 at UFC 333 in Abu Dhabi. MMA Junkie says it's his ninth title-fight win, passing José Aldo's record, and it earned him a $100K bonus.
