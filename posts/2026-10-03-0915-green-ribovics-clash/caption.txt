UPDATE: King Green and Esteban Ribovics nearly came to blows outside the UFC 332 weigh-ins, hours before they fight in Salt Lake City.
King Green or Ribovics? 👇

Still angry that Ribovics came in half a pound heavy, Green and his friend, pro boxer Arnold Barboza Jr., confronted Ribovics and his team after Friday's weigh-ins. Insults flew and Green told them he'd fight right there before things cooled down. Green, 40, made weight at 155.5 and takes 20% of Ribovics' purse. He's never missed weight in 31 UFC fights, and he made it clear the cut wasn't easy. The lightweight bout is on tonight's UFC 332 main card, with a possible BMF title shot on the line for Green.
