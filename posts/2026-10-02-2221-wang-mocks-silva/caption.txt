Wang Cong mocked Natalia Silva to her face at the UFC 332 ceremonial weigh-ins, and Silva shoved her away.

In her trademark Joker-style makeup, Wang squared up and kept bouncing her hips, imitating the hip feints Silva used to beat Rose Namajunas. Silva pushed Wang's arms aside and security split them up. The two meet Saturday night in Salt Lake City for the vacant UFC women's flyweight title, a title fight many fans had written off as a quiet main event.

Did Wang just get in Silva's head, or wake her up?
