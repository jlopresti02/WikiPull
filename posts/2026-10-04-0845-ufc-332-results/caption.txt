Natália Silva is the new UFC women's flyweight champion after a unanimous decision win over Wang Cong in the UFC 332 main event. 🏆
Best finish of the night: Talbott or Walker? 👇

UFC 332 in Salt Lake City set modern-era records with 11 finishes, nine of them first-round knockouts. Payton Talbott stopped former champ Deiveson Figueiredo in round one of the co-main, Esteban Ribovics finished King Green, and Johnny Walker landed a knee KO in his heavyweight debut. Kopylov, Rodriguez, Pinas and Walker took home Performance of the Night bonuses.
