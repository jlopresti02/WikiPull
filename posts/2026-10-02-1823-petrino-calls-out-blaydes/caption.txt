Vitor Petrino wants Curtis Blaydes' spot in the UFC heavyweight rankings, and he's asking for the fight at UFC 335.

The No. 9-ranked Brazilian is 4-0 since moving up from light heavyweight, most recently beating Serghei Spivac in August. He says he's told the UFC he wants No. 7 Blaydes for the December 12 card in Las Vegas and is waiting on an answer. Blaydes (20-6) is coming off a win over Waldo Cortes-Acosta in September.
