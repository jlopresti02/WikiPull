Iasmin Lucindo is back from suspension, and she has a Brazilian rival waiting at UFC 333.

Lucindo (18-6) meets Nicolle Caliari (9-4) in a strawweight fight added to UFC 333 in Abu Dhabi, per MMA Junkie. Lucindo returns after a nine-month suspension for mesterolone; the UFC's anti-doping program accepted that a contaminated supplement was to blame. Caliari is coming off a submission win over Shauna Bannon in May.

UFC 333 is headlined by Alexander Volkanovski vs. Movsar Evloev and Petr Yan vs. Merab Dvalishvili.
