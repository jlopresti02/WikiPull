UPDATE: Deiveson Figueiredo got under Payton Talbott's skin again, this time with a fake punch at the UFC 332 ceremonial weigh-ins.

Days after shoving Talbott at the press conference, the former UFC flyweight champion faked a strike during their final faceoff, Talbott reacted and UFC staff stepped in. The two meet in a five-round bantamweight co-main event Saturday in Salt Lake City, with Talbott favored over the 37-year-old Brazilian.
