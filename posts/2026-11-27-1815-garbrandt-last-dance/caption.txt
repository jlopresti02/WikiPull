Former UFC champ Cody Garbrandt says his next fight will be his last.
Farewell win or not? 👇

Garbrandt told MMA Junkie he's targeting the UFC's February card in Cleveland as his farewell, with one fight left on his deal and no opponent named yet. The Ohio native took the bantamweight title from Dominick Cruz at UFC 207 in 2016.
