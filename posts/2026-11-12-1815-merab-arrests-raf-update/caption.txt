UPDATE: Abu Dhabi Police say 10 people were arrested over the hotel fight involving Merab Dvalishvili and Usman Nurmagomedov.
Police say 8 were held for fighting and 2 for filming, and the case went to the Public Prosecution. At Thursday's RAF Abu Dhabi press conference, RAF said Usman won't attend Saturday's event. No decision yet on Merab's match with Marlon Moraes.

Hoping this is resolved safely for everyone involved.
