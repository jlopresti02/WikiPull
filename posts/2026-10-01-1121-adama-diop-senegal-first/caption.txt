Adama Diop just made UFC history for Senegal. 🇸🇳

The heavyweight earned a UFC contract on Dana White's Contender Series on Sept. 30, stopping Zaurbek Sabanov by TKO at 2:13 of the second round after mixing in a takedown and clinch strikes. That makes him the first fighter from Senegal ever signed by the UFC.

Diop, a former Senegalese traditional wrestler, is now 6-1 in MMA and came in as the EFC heavyweight champion. His UFC debut date hasn't been announced yet. Big UFC signing news for African MMA.
