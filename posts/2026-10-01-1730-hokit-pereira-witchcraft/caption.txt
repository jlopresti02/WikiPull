Josh Hokit is not letting the Alex Pereira 'witchcraft' talk go.

On the Believe Me You podcast, the unbeaten UFC heavyweight (10-0) again claimed Pereira's famous 'Chama' chant is shaman witchcraft and took personal shots at the former two-division champ. Jiri Prochazka made similar claims before their fights, and Pereira's camp has said he does not practice witchcraft; 'Chama' is Portuguese for roughly 'let's go.' Hokit wants Pereira after he faces Ciryl Gane for the heavyweight title at UFC 334 at Madison Square Garden on November 14. Dana White is set to reveal Pereira's next fight on the UFC 332 stream this weekend. 🔮

Trash talk or a real rivalry brewing?
