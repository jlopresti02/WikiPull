The UFC just released No. 3 strawweight Virna Jandiroba, 13 months after her title fight.
Fair or not? 👇

Jandiroba (23-5) shared the news on Instagram, saying it caught her off guard and she isn't retiring. She lost the vacant title fight to Mackenzie Dern last year and dropped a decision to Tatiana Suarez on Nov 7.
