Unbeaten bantamweight Farid Basharat is out of the UFC at 16-0.
Mistake or fair? 👇

His contract ran out after his UFC 329 win over John Garza and the UFC let him go, making the 7-0 UFC fighter a free agent, per Guilherme Cruz. His brother Javid already left for the PFL.
