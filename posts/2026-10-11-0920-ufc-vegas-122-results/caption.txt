Brendan Allen choked out Christian Leroy Duncan late in round 5 to win the UFC Vegas 122 main event. 🔥
Best finish: Fili or Tanzilovi? 👇

The rear-naked choke at 2:46 of the fifth made it four straight UFC wins for Allen. Jai Herbert outpointed Matheus Camilo in the co-main, and Andre Fili and Otari Tanzilovi both finished with head kicks. Allen, Fili, Tanzilovi and Alice Pereira each took home $100K bonuses.
