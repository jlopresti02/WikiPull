Esteban Ribovics says an air conditioner is why he missed weight for his UFC 332 fight with King Green.
Buy it or not? 👇

Ribovics said the AC was switched on in the workout room during his cut and he stopped sweating, leaving him half a pound over the lightweight limit. He laughed off talk of sabotage and said his issue was with boxer Arnold Barboza Jr., not Bobby Green, whom he stopped in the first round in Salt Lake City. UFC news and UFC 332 aftermath.
