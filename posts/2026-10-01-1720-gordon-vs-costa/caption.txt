Melquizael Costa is jumping up to lightweight, and Jared Gordon is waiting for him at Madison Square Garden.

UFC 334 in New York on Saturday, November 14 just added Gordon vs. Costa at 155 pounds. Costa, the Brazilian ranked No. 12 at featherweight, moves up after his six-fight win streak ended in a main-event loss to Arnold Allen. New York native Gordon is looking to snap a two-fight skid after losing to Jim Miller earlier this year. The bout joins a stacked UFC 334 card headlined by Ciryl Gane vs. Josh Hokit for the interim heavyweight title. 🥊

Does Costa carry his power up a weight class, or does Gordon get it done at home?
