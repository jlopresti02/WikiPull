Umar Nurmagomedov is taking on Urijah Faber on the wrestling mat.

The UFC bantamweight makes his Real American Freestyle debut against the Hall of Famer at RAF Abu Dhabi on November 14 at Etihad Arena. It will be Faber's third RAF match. The card is headlined by Khamzat Chimaev vs. Colby Covington for the RAF cruiserweight crossover title, and it's the first event in a five-year deal that brings RAF freestyle wrestling to Abu Dhabi.

Can Faber hang with a Nurmagomedov on the mat?
