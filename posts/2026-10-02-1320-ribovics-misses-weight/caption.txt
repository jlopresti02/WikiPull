UPDATE: Esteban Ribovics missed weight for his UFC 332 fight with King Green, and Green's BMF title dream is on the line.

Ribovics hit the scale at 156.5 pounds on Friday morning in Salt Lake City, half a pound over the lightweight limit, and will forfeit part of his purse. Dana White has said Bobby "King" Green deserves a BMF title shot if he wins Saturday. Everyone else on the card made weight, so the Natalia Silva vs. Wang Cong flyweight title fight is official.
