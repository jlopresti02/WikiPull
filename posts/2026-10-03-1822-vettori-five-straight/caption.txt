Marvin Vettori's losing streak hit five fights after Ismail Naurdiev beat him at UFC 332.
Cut or keep him? 👇

Naurdiev took a unanimous decision, 29-28 on all three cards, in a middleweight fight in Salt Lake City that saw an accidental eye poke in round one and both men hurt in round two. The bout was first set for June before Vettori withdrew with a rib injury. The former UFC middleweight title challenger now faces big questions about his future. UFC 332 results and MMA news.
