Islam Makhachev has reportedly pulled out of his UFC 336 welterweight title defense against Carlos Prates.
Wait for Islam or interim belt? 👇

Ag Fight broke the news, no reason has been given yet, and Dana White only called the report "interesting." UFC 336 is set for January 30 in Toronto, and there's no word yet on Prates' next opponent.
