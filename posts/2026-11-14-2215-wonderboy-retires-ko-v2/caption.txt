Stephen 'Wonderboy' Thompson just retired with a spinning wheel kick KO of Charles Radtke.
Greatest exit ever: yes or no? 👇

Thompson finished Radtke at 2:54 of round 3 at UFC 334 in New York, then made his MMA retirement official after almost 15 years in the UFC. He walks away 18-9-1.

🔥 Hot take: No UFC fighter has ever retired better than Wonderboy.
