UPDATE: Usman Nurmagomedov is out of prison in Abu Dhabi after his sentence over the hotel attack on Merab Dvalishvili was reduced.
Abu Dhabi's judicial department says the eight defendants' fines were halved to Dh150,000 each, citing reconciliation and Merab's own appeal for their release. Reports say Khabib Nurmagomedov helped negotiate it, though that hasn't been confirmed.
